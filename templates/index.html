What is Linear Regression?

Linear Regression is a fundamental machine learning algorithm that models the relationship between a dependent variable (like House Price) and one or more independent variables (like Area, Bedrooms). It draws a "line of best fit" through historical data points, allowing it to predict prices for new, unseen houses by looking at where they fall on that line.
